Some very cool new screenshots of Lyn in her Memory Blade outfit!

I did a lot of shader work to enhance the graphics. At the moment these changes are expensive, so I got to optimise them. First I want to use an existing profiling framework I found in the marketplace, then I'll use AI to do some A/B testing in order to get a grasp what exactly is expensive.

I'm very happy so far with the development, and the more time passes the more I know the definite direction of my game. It's still going to be a mix of NieR, KH and Zelda, but with unique mechanics I thought of before sleeping or while showering, lol ¯⁠\\⁠\_⁠(⁠ツ⁠)⁠\_⁠/⁠¯

I'm going to show gameplay soon and also post my devlog video on Youtube.
