Lately I have been working on the music of my game! I'm sharing here a glimpse of what I was composing; I'm thinking of using this as some of the more calmer and mysterious areas in the world of Proto:ReCompute

How did I compose this? I just sat on the piano, thought of a key (here it's C minor) and started playing. I usually always start with the left hand for the accompanied piece and then the melody on the right. Then... I just play it 💚.
